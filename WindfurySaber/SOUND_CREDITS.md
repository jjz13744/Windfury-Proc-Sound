Bundled Audio Credits and Provenance
====================================
Verified 2026-11-14. All 20 files are mono Ogg Vorbis at 44,100 Hz.
Third-party assets retain their original public-domain / CC0 status,
not the addon source-code license. No movie, commercial game or Wago clip is
included, and this pack is not claimed to match any particular Wago sound.

1. Wolf Howl (wolf_howl.ogg)
   Original: "Wolf howls" by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service.
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wolf_howls.ogg
   Original archived file:
   https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/87/Wolf_howls.ogg
   Public domain: official-duty U.S. federal government work (PD-USGov-FWS),
   as specifically identified on the file's archival source page.
   Source file SHA1: 44ce16c609c16ce8ae05b4da61179bf6c9c4ce09 (verified).
   Modified: first 2.8s excerpt, mono conversion, 44.1kHz resampling,
   level adjustment, fades and Vorbis encoding. Genuine wolf recording.
   No U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service endorsement is implied.

2. Chicken (chicken.ogg)
   Original: "Chicken Single Alarm Call" by Rudmer_Rotteveel.
   https://freesound.org/people/Rudmer_Rotteveel/sounds/316920/
   CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
   Derived from the author's public high-quality preview of an original
   recording of one of the author's chickens. Modified: mono conversion,
   near-silence trimming, level adjustment, fades and encoding.

3. Sci-fi blade (lightsaber.ogg)
   Original procedural waveform created for this addon from oscillators and
   filtered noise. No third-party recording or sample was used. The waveform
   and its generation script may be used, modified and redistributed without
   restriction. It is a generic buzzing energy-blade effect, not the sound
   from a film, game, commercial sound library or Wago aura.
   Reproducible source: tools/prepare_audio.py.
